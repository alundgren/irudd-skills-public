Grounded palette

Warm paper ground, Grounded brown as the hue anchor, one saturated accent. This page is painted in the palette it describes.

Warmth and low saturation carry the identity. An app may derive a light or dark ground from the same rule. This screen uses the warm paper ground.

What changed

  • The number carries weight. 600 and tabular. It is the one thing the task needs, so it gets the emphasis. Visual weight follows frequency of use.
  • A "kcal / 100 g" label above the column. The only structural change. Without it the number is unlabelled and you only know what it means because you built it.
  • Alternating row tint instead of the grey stripe. One lightness step on the anchor hue, no colour change.
  • The search field is the lightest thing on the page. 96% lightness against a 91% ground. It is used on every visit, so it reads as the place to act.

What did not change

Row order, the bolded matched substring, one number per row, right aligned. That was all correct already.

The derivation rule

An app that follows these six lines looks like family even when it picks different values. This is the part that has to survive a game overlay, a cooking site and an admin panel.

  • Hue anchor 25. Grounded's hue. Every neutral, background, surface, border, muted text, sits on hue 25 or within a few degrees. That is what makes the greys warm without anyone choosing a warm grey.
  • Saturation 20% to 26% below 75% lightness. Above 75% go up to 60%, where saturation reads as warmth rather than colour. Napery is the proof: 59% saturation and it still looks like paper.
  • Supporting hues near 105 and 200. Sage and greyed blue, a third and two thirds of the way round from the anchor. Spread, not clustered. The shared saturation ceiling is what makes them one family instead of three unrelated colours.
  • One saturated accent, hue 33 at 44% to 52%. Bosc Pear. That is the accent budget for a whole screen.
  • Roles separate by lightness, never by saturation. Background, surface, raised and border are four steps 4 to 5 lightness points apart on the anchor hue.
  • Primary text at 7:1, plus or minus 0.8. Reach it by moving lightness apart. Never by heading for white or black. Secondary text at 4.5:1 or better, and only for genuinely secondary information.

Why Grounded is the anchor

Grounded is #785B47 at 37% lightness. Creamy on Grounded measures 5.1:1, so that pair cannot provide both the page background and the primary text target. Grounded supplies the hue instead. The neutral roles move along that hue until the required contrast is reached.

The default ground is light but not white. At 91% lightness it reads as warm paper without producing the glare of a pure white background.
